FreedomHotspot is a Wi-Fi captive portal platform — guest authentication, marketing capture, hardware integration and visitor analytics for venues running public Wi-Fi.

The portal handles vendor-specific access-point protocols, RADIUS authentication and session management on behalf of the venue, so operators can focus on the guest experience and the marketing relationship rather than the underlying networking.

## What we do

- **Branded captive portals** — venue-specific login pages with custom theming, terms of service, and welcome flows.
- **Multi-vendor hardware integration** — one platform across access points and gateways from many vendors (full list below), avoiding lock-in to a single hardware ecosystem.
- **Guest authentication** — social login (Facebook), email signup, pin-code access, and returning-visitor recognition.
- **Marketing capture & follow-up** — email opt-ins, scheduled marketing campaigns, and visitor data export.
- **Visitor analytics** — session counts, dwell times, returning-visitor metrics, exportable reports.
- **Multi-site management** — one admin account, many venues; per-site branding and data segregation.

## Who it's for

Hotels, cafés, restaurants, leisure and retail venues — single-site operators and multi-site groups alike. The platform is delivered as a managed service rather than a product to install.

## Hardware we support

Captive-portal integration for access points and gateways from:

- Cambium Networks (cnPilot, XV-series)
- Cisco Meraki
- MikroTik (RouterOS)
- OpenMesh / Datto
- TP-Link Omada
- Keenetic
- Generic RADIUS-compatible hardware
